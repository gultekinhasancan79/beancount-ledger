## Outcome

All sixteen recorded episodes terminated with `piv_submitted`, achieved reward 1.0
and global completion, and resolved the selected item. Each arm wrote exactly one
ledger revision. No provider failure or incomplete pair was recorded. Across eight
model–case pairs covering four company-months, there were zero treatment-specific
selected-item failures.

Observed completion differences were zero. Effort was not uniform: GLM-5.2's Falcon
treatment used 39,844 output tokens and 594 s against 21,698 and 379 s for its
control. Single observations; they do not establish an effort effect, but the
measurements cannot be described as uniformly unchanged.

All arms submitted before the episode ceiling; output usage ranged from 6,092 to
39,844 tokens. The stored final consecutive-truncation counter was zero and the
no-tool-turn counters were unset in every row, consistent with every turn having
called a tool. These summary records do not establish the absence of every
intermediate truncation.

**Ledger inspection (author's, on the archived bytes).** Each selected payment
posts to `Assets:Bank:Checking` and `Liabilities:AP`, with the erroneous
counteraccount removed from that payment. Other legitimate account uses and chart
declarations are preserved. The sixteen workspaces (public inputs and final
outputs) are archived under `reviews/wa1b_screen_2026-09-08/workspaces/<model>/<case>/<arm>/`;
all sixteen final-ledger SHA-256 values match the `final_ledger_sha256` recorded in
the rows. The evidence directory `reviews/wa1b_screen_2026-09-08/` also holds the 16 arm rows + 8 pair records
(wa1b_screen.jsonl, each row with `config_key`), the per-subject and chain logs, the
pool, the adjudication records, and the selector / adjudicator / runner / builder /
checker scripts as run.

## Decision

Defer further development and confirmation of this wrong-account construction.
The screen supplied no completion-based reason to prioritise it. This is consistent
with the earlier retain/defer heuristic (pre-declared: treatment-specific failures on at
least three company-months, controls fine, no budget/protocol explanation → retain;
otherwise defer), but this screen replaced the larger design first specified; that
original design was not completed.

The study does not establish equivalence, absence of an effort effect, or
performance outside these cases, requested model IDs and instrument settings.

## Limitations

Four cases; incomplete crossing of models and cases; one deployment route and one
cap; no within-cell replication; no generated-world or training-utility
conclusion. The records attest requested model IDs, not immutable served
checkpoints. The rescore is a same-scorer consistency check, not independent
accounting validation; `config_key` binds model, route, digest and caps, not the
case bytes, builder or schedule.
